Question 1: What are some Pros and Cons of Agile Development

Pros of Agile Development:
- Able to get early and continuos feedback from user
- Highly flexible, team is able to pivot directions if needed mroe easily

Cons of Agile Development:
- If user or stakeholder is not very active in the development, software may not turn out as expected
- Low predictability in terms of budget and finalized timelines

Question 2: Role and Responsibilities of Scrum Master vs Product Owner

The Product Owner is the person who decides what to work on and why. 
They set the goals and direction for the team, and handle more stakeholder communication. 
The product owner is overall in charge of the product vision more.

On the other hand, the Scrum Master is the one that helps the team work efficiently using agile methodologies. 
They are in charge of the more scrum related tasks like JIRA tooling, sprint management, and ensuring teamamtes are able to meet properly.
The scrum master is overall in charge of team efficiency.
