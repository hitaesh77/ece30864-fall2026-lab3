Question 1: What are some Pros and Cons of Agile Development

Pros of Agile Development:
- Able to get early and continuos feedback from user
- Highly flexible, team is able to pivot directions if needed mroe easily

Cons of Agile Development:
- If user or stakeholder is not very active in the development, software may not turn out as expected
- Low predictability in terms of budget and finalized timelines


Question 2: Role and Responsibilities of Scrum Master vs Product Owner

The Product Owner is the person who decides what to work on and why. 
They set the goals and direction for the team, and handle more stakeholder communication. 
The product owner is overall in charge of the product vision more.

On the other hand, the Scrum Master is the one that helps the team work efficiently using agile methodologies. 
They are in charge of the more scrum related tasks like JIRA tooling, sprint management, and ensuring teamamtes are able to meet properly.
The scrum master is overall in charge of team efficiency.


Question 3: 3 Types of Meetings during Sprint Cycle

Sprint Planning Meetings:
- Define what needs to be completed in the upcoming sprint
- Formalize story points and assign stories to team

Daily Standup Meetings:
- An opportunity for team members to discuss work completed, work planned for the day, and any blockers
- Usually a short meeting for progress updates, so if there is a discussion requiring more time, there is usually an opportuntiy here for round 2 discussions, or scheduling time for a formal meeting

Sprint Retrospective Meetings:
- An end of sprint meeting to talk about the work completed this sprint, and move incomplete items to the backlog
- An opportuntiy to reflect on the sprint, think about what went well, and what could be improved, and best next steps for moving forward


Question 4: Epic vs Story vs Bug vs Task

Epic:
- A big goal or large body of work that will span many sprints
- This larger goal or product gets broken down into stories, tasks, bugs, etc...

Story:
- A software requirement that is described concisely
- It can usually be completed in one sprint, and pertains to a larger goal in an Epic

Bug:
- An error in a feature that needs to be fixed
- Tracked so team is aware of current progress and blockers, and so team members know what might be broken or needs to be fixed

Task:
- A general piece of work that needs to be done 
- In Jira, stories and tasks are of equal level, unless a story has specific subtasks connected to it
