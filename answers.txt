Question 1: What are some Pros and Cons of Agile Development

Pros of Agile Development:
- Able to get early and continuos feedback from user
- Highly flexible, team is able to pivot directions if needed mroe easily

Cons of Agile Development:
- If user or stakeholder is not very active in the development, software may not turn out as expected
- Low predictability in terms of budget and finalized timelines
